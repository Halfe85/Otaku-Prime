Otaku Prime 0.1.2 Alpha8b
=========================

Alpha8b supersedes the abandoned resolver/library direction from Alpha8.

- Define raw watchlist ingestion as Prime's current application boundary.
- Keep local administrator login and forced first-password replacement.
- Keep AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, and Simkl account connection flows.
- Fetch each connected provider into the canonical provider-tagged `watchlist_items` table.
- Preserve provider IDs, titles, list status, progress, episode count, format, release date, adult flag, and original provider JSON.
- Show the raw multi-provider table in Watchlist Management with responsive paging, search, and status filtering.
- Refresh raw snapshots at startup, on supported periodic intervals, and after account connection changes.
- Remove the TMDB and TheTVDB configuration dependency and mandatory metadata gate.
- Remove franchise/relation resolution, metadata structure resolution, catalogue promotion, and watchlist mediation.
- Remove Kodi inventory, ownership reconciliation, direct database projection, and watch-status projection.
- Transactionally remove obsolete resolver/placement columns from existing `watchlist_items` tables while preserving raw fetched rows.
