Otaku Prime 0.1.2 Alpha6
========================

AniList franchise resolution:
- Ignore manga and one-shot PREQUEL relations when resolving anime franchises.
- Exclude movie and special relations from TV-season chain numbering.
- Limit numbered season-chain traversal to TV and TV Short records; route ONA and OVA records through the season-zero mapping path.
- Allocate the next available local season number for branching franchises.
- Reconcile season-number collisions left by earlier partial imports instead of failing.

Watchlist management:
- Added view-sized client-side pagination to the Watchlist Management table.
- Change watchlist pages instantly without fetching the list again.

Kodi virtual library:
- Moved generated stream files to special://profile/library/ in Kodi userdata.
- Keep Kodi's existing video database and leave advancedsettings.xml unchanged.
- Scan the movie and TV source roots separately using Kodi JSON-RPC.
- Register the generated Movies and TV Shows folders in sources.xml while preserving existing Kodi sources and creating a one-time backup before changing the file.
- Report when Kodi must be restarted to load newly registered sources.

AniList media categories:
- Retain AniList format and release date throughout watchlist staging and promotion.
- Store media format, relation/category, and optional verified secondary-provider identity on seasons.
- Distinguish TV, ONA, OVA, movie, special, and spin-off entries. AniList exposes OAD/ODA releases as OVA rather than a separate format.
- Target movie and special-format entries at Kodi season 00, but keep them unpublished until a verified TMDB/TVDB episode mapping prevents S00E01 collisions.

Testing:
- Added coverage for non-anime PREQUEL relations.
- Added coverage for two franchise branches sharing the same relation depth.
- Added coverage for AniList category/date retention, safe season-zero staging, and idempotent Kodi source registration.
